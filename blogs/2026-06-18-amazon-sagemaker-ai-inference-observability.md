Amazon SageMaker AI introduces observability for inference endpoints, providing visibility into token performance, GPU health, inference component placement, and autoscaling behavior via a pre-built CloudWatch dashboard. Customers can connect Grafana directly using PromQL endpoints to diagnose issues in minutes rather than hours.
