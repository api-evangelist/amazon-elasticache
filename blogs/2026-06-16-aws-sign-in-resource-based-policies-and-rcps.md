AWS Sign-in now supports resource-based policies and resource control policies (RCPs) for the Management Console, restricting console sign-in to expected networks. Resource-based policies apply to individual accounts while RCPs apply organization-wide, combining with Console Private Access to control both networks and accounts users can access.
