AWS Transform offers a model-to-model migration custom transformation that assesses generative AI workloads and produces a migration plan for moving from third-party providers to Amazon Bedrock. The agent scans the codebase, identifies AI SDKs and models, and maps models to Bedrock equivalents with transparent cost comparisons.
