Nested virtualization is now available on additional Intel platforms including C7i, R7i, M7i, C8id, R8id, and M8id variants, extending to US GovCloud (US-East) and US GovCloud (US-West). Customers can run KVM or Hyper-V nested environments on EC2 for emulators, hardware simulation, and Windows Subsystem for Linux workloads.
