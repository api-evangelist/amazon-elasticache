AWS announces general availability of a new Local Zone in Hanoi, Vietnam, supporting Amazon EC2 C7i, M7i, and R7i instances, Amazon S3 One Zone-Infrequent Access storage, and Amazon EBS with Local Snapshots. It helps customers meet data residency requirements and supports AI/ML inference workloads, and is available in more than 30 metropolitan areas worldwide.
