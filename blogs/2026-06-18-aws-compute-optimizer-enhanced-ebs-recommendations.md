AWS Compute Optimizer now includes improved visibility into IOPS and throughput spikes for EBS volume rightsizing, analyzing VolumeIOPSExceededCheck and VolumeThroughputExceededCheck metrics. It is available in all AWS Regions except GovCloud and China, with CloudWatch metrics at no additional charge for Nitro-based EC2 instances.
