AWS HealthOmics now streams workflow engine logs to CloudWatch in real-time, enabling monitoring of workflow orchestration events, task scheduling details, import/export activity, and full stack traces on errors. CloudWatch alarms detect anomalies early and dashboards integrate with existing observability tooling.
