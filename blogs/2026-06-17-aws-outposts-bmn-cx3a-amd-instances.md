AWS announces availability of bmn-cx3a instances on second-generation Outposts racks, the first AMD-based instances with accelerated networking on Outposts. They feature 5th Gen AMD EPYC processors at 4.1 GHz and NVIDIA ConnectX-7 cards, delivering up to 800 Gbps bare-metal accelerated bandwidth.
