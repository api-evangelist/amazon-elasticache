Amazon MSK Provisioned clusters with Express brokers now support Intelligent Rebalancing on existing clusters at no additional cost, optimally rebalancing Kafka resources without customers managing partitions. It performs operations up to 180 times faster compared to Standard brokers and is continuously monitored for resource imbalance.
