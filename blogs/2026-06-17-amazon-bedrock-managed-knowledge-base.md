Amazon Bedrock Managed Knowledge Base, a fully managed retrieval-augmented generation (RAG) service, is now generally available, enabling production-ready AI agents grounded in enterprise data without managing vector databases or pipelines. Six native connectors support Amazon S3, SharePoint, Confluence, Google Drive, OneDrive, and Web Crawler with automatic syncing.
