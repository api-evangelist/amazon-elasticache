Amazon Bedrock AgentCore now supports Guardrails in policy, evaluating outputs of authorized agent actions and tool calls in real-time to detect and block prompt injection attacks, harmful content, and sensitive information exposure. Policy evaluations occur at the gateway perimeter for consistent enforcement regardless of agent autonomy.
